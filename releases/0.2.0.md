# Meet Meet 0.2.0

For **Apple silicon Macs running macOS 26 or newer**.

## What's new

- Click an upcoming or ongoing countdown to open meeting info without consuming
  a later reminder. Join opens the meeting link and closes info on success.
  Conferencing links in locations and notes are recognized too.
- A unified **Settings → Cues** tab groups independent before-start and
  before-end visual and audio reminders, with previews and custom media imports.
  Imported files are copied into the app so moving the originals won't break cues.
- Visual cues default to one minute before start and two minutes before end,
  with new phase-specific artwork. Meeting info stays open through meeting start
  until dismissed, the Join link opens or the meeting ends.
- Bundled sounds are phase-specific: **Meet Meet** for start, **Wrap Wrap** for
  end. System and imported sounds work for either; audio remains off by default.
- Optionally shorten Google Workspace structured room names to just the room
  name in meeting details. Enable it in **Settings → General**.

## Fixes and improvements

- Preserve the countdown stack's preferred anchor through display relocation,
  title expansion and native movement sequences, including failed placement saves.
- Keep menu phase labels and countdown actions on one reconciled snapshot, and
  revalidate reminder actions against current meeting data and time when clicked.
- Allow manual meeting info when saved cue dismissal history is unreadable,
  while keeping automatic visual cues safely disabled.
- Recover unsupported opposite-phase saved bundled sounds to the appropriate
  default without changing cue timing or enablement.
- Updated setup documentation, screenshots and stable latest-download assets.

## Install or update

Download **MeetMeet-0.2.0-arm64.dmg**, open it, and drag **MeetMeet.app** to
Applications. Quit an older copy before replacing it, then launch the replacement.
Look for the calendar icon in the menu bar, not the Dock.

**This build is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or Apple-notarized.**
macOS may block first launch. After trying to open it, use **System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** only if you trust the download. Managed Macs
may prohibit overrides. Do not disable Gatekeeper.

Settings should remain saved, but calendar access and launch-at-login approval
may need to be granted again. Updates are manual; there is no automatic updater.
See the [installation and update guide](https://github.com/nnerik/meet-meet/blob/v0.2.0/docs/installation.md).
The matching SHA-256 asset checks download integrity, not publisher identity.

## Known limitations

- No Intel support or support for macOS versions before 26.
- Calendar-provider identity changes may require hiding a meeting again.
- Sounds and Spaces/full-screen visibility are best-effort within macOS rules.
- Automated geometry, native-window and packaging checks do not establish every
  physical display, pointer, VoiceOver, Gatekeeper, permission or login scenario.
